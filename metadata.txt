name=Ukiyo-e Relief
qgisMinimumVersion=3.22
description=Renders a DEM as a Japanese woodblock print (ukiyo-e): flat colour plates, bokashi, key-block outlines, misregistration, wood grain and washi paper. Works on any platform; no extra software required.
about=Deterministic, parameter-driven rendering of digital elevation models in the style of Japanese woodblock prints. Two modes: a georeferenced plan view (GeoTIFF) and an oblique layered landscape with aerial perspective (PNG). Everything is computed from the DEM by an algorithm; no AI is involved and the same seed always gives the same print.
    Dependencies: only libraries shipped with QGIS (numpy, GDAL, matplotlib). SciPy is used when present to speed up labelling, but is optional. No separate installation is needed.

    Plan view (GeoTIFF) and layered landscape (PNG) modes; automatic sea detection; Hokusai and Hiroshige palettes; silhouette-only key-block outlines; relief curve and automatic vertical exaggeration; snow following gullies; simulated woodblock printing.
